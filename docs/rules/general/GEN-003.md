**Rule.** Real patient data is never sent to an AI model unless there is a privacy contract with that provider, and never to dev or pre environments. Prompts, tests, fixtures and seeds use synthetic data only.

**Why.** Health data is sensitive and legally protected; free or unvetted providers may retain prompts.

**Correct.** `FullName = "Test Patient 1"`, document `1234567`.

**Incorrect.** Pasting a production row into a prompt to debug it.
