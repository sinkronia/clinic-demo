**Rule.** Commit messages and PR titles follow Conventional Commits (`feat:`, `fix:`, `chore:`, `docs:`, `test:`, `refactor:`), in English.

**Why.** Readable history and automatic changelogs.

**Correct.** `feat: schedule appointments without overlaps`

**Incorrect.** `updated stuff`, `agrego citas`.

**Exception.** None. (commitlint will verify it later, hence `proposed`.)
