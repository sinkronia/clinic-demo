**Rule.** `Nullable` is `enable` and `TreatWarningsAsErrors` is `true` in every project. Do not override either in a `.csproj`.

**Why.** Null bugs and ignored warnings are cheap to stop at compile time and expensive in production.

**Correct.** Model absence with `string?`; fix the warning at its source.

**Incorrect.** `<Nullable>disable</Nullable>`, `#pragma warning disable`, or the null-forgiving `!` to silence a real warning.

**Exception.** `!` is acceptable in tests when the preceding assertion proves non-null.
